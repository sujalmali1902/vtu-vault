Privacy Policy for VTU Vault

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

VTU Vault ("we", "our", "us", or "the App") is an educational application designed to provide study materials and educational resources for engineering students.

This Privacy Policy explains how VTU Vault handles information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

VTU Vault does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise location
  • User account information
  • User-uploaded files

VTU Vault does not require users to create an account to access its educational content.

The app does not intentionally access or collect files stored on the user's device.

2. Advertising

VTU Vault uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and support the development and maintenance of the application.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from a user's device. Depending on the SDK configuration and advertising features being used, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information related to advertisements displayed or interacted with

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through VTU Vault and its advertising services may be used to:

  • Provide educational content and app functionality
  • Display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Support and improve advertising services

VTU Vault does not sell users' personal information.

4. Third-Party Service - Google AdMob

VTU Vault uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

VTU Vault is an educational application intended primarily for engineering students and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because advertisements are provided through Google AdMob, Google's advertising and consent mechanisms may apply depending on the user's location and circumstances.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by VTU Vault.

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's documentation.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

VTU Vault does not maintain a user account database or intentionally store users' personal information.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is subject to the respective third party's policies and retention practices.

Because VTU Vault does not create user accounts or maintain a personal-information database, there is no user account that needs to be deleted.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
